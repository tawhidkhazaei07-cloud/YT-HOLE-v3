    How it works

    Four steps, about ten seconds

    1. 1

      Copy a permitted URL

      Grab the link to a video you own or are allowed to save.

    2. 2

      Paste it into YT Hole

      Drop it in the field and press Analyze.

    3. 3

      Choose quality and format

      Pick resolution and file type. The size updates live.

    4. 4

      Download the file

      Watch the progress ring fill and save your file.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    YT Hole is a simple web tool that turns a video link into a downloadable file in the quality and format you choose.

    You paste a link, YT Hole reads the available streams, you choose a quality and format, and the file is prepared for you to save.

    YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok links are supported. Private, age-restricted, or protected content is not.

    Video as MP4 or WEBM from 144p up to the best available quality, and audio-only as MP3.

    Speed depends on the file size, the quality you chose, your connection, and how quickly the source platform responds. Higher resolutions take longer.

    Files are saved by your browser, usually in your Downloads folder. YT Hole keeps only a history entry, not the file itself.

    Yes. YT Hole works in any modern mobile browser on iPhone and Android.
